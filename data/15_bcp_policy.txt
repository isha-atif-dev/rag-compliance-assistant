Meridian Financial Services Ltd
BUSINESS CONTINUITY AND DISASTER RECOVERY POLICY
Document Owner: Operational Resilience | Version 2.0 | Last Reviewed: April 2025

1. Purpose
This policy ensures the firm can continue critical operations, or recover quickly, in the event of a significant disruption.

2. Business Impact Analysis
Critical business services are identified and assigned a maximum tolerable outage (MTO), reviewed annually.

3. Recovery Objectives
3.1 Recovery Time Objective (RTO) for core payment processing: 4 hours.
3.2 Recovery Point Objective (RPO) for customer data: 15 minutes, achieved through continuous database replication.

4. Testing
Business continuity plans are tested at least annually through simulated exercises, including a full failover test of core systems.

5. Communication Plan
In the event of a major incident, a designated Incident Communications Lead is responsible for updating customers, staff, and regulators as required.

6. Regulatory Notification
Where an incident materially disrupts customer-facing services, the FCA must be notified as soon as possible and in any case within the timelines set out in the firm's Operational Resilience framework.
