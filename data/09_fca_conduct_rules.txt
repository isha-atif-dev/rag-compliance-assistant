Meridian Financial Services Ltd
FCA INDIVIDUAL CONDUCT RULES SUMMARY
Document Owner: HR & Compliance | Version 1.4 | Last Reviewed: October 2025

1. Purpose
This document summarises the FCA's Individual Conduct Rules that apply to all staff under the Senior Managers and Certification Regime (SMCR).

2. The Individual Conduct Rules
Rule 1: You must act with integrity.
Rule 2: You must act with due skill, care, and diligence.
Rule 3: You must be open and cooperative with the FCA, PRA, and other regulators.
Rule 4: You must pay due regard to the interests of customers and treat them fairly.
Rule 5: You must observe proper standards of market conduct.

3. Additional Rules for Senior Managers
SM1: You must take reasonable steps to ensure the business you are responsible for is controlled effectively.
SM2: You must take reasonable steps to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements in your area.
SM3: You must disclose appropriately any information the FCA or PRA would reasonably expect notice of.
SM4: You must take reasonable steps to ensure any delegation of responsibilities is to an appropriate person, with appropriate oversight.

4. Application and Training
All staff must complete Conduct Rules training within 30 days of starting and confirm annually that they understand and will abide by the rules.

5. Breach Reporting
Suspected or confirmed breaches of the Conduct Rules must be reported to HR and Compliance. Confirmed breaches may result in disciplinary action and are reportable to the FCA where they meet the notification threshold.
