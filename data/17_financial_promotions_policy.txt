Meridian Financial Services Ltd
FINANCIAL PROMOTIONS APPROVAL POLICY
Document Owner: Marketing Compliance | Version 1.7 | Last Reviewed: January 2026

1. Purpose
This policy ensures all financial promotions are fair, clear, and not misleading, in accordance with FCA rules (FSMA s.21 and COBS).

2. Approval Requirement
No financial promotion (marketing material, app messaging, social media content, or advertising) may be published unless approved by a person with appropriate competence, per FCA authorisation requirements.

3. Approval Process
3.1 Marketing submits proposed promotions via the Compliance Approval System with at least 3 business days' lead time.
3.2 The reviewer checks for balanced risk/benefit presentation, accurate representation of product features, and required risk warnings.
3.3 High-risk promotions (e.g. involving investment products) require sign-off from a senior Compliance Officer.

4. Record Keeping
Approved promotions, including the version history and approver, are retained for 5 years from the date of last use.

5. Post-Publication Monitoring
Live promotions are periodically re-reviewed to ensure continued accuracy, particularly where product terms have changed.

6. Breaches
Any promotion published without proper approval must be withdrawn immediately and reported to Compliance for root cause review.
