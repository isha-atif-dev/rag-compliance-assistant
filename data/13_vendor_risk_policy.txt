Meridian Financial Services Ltd
THIRD-PARTY VENDOR RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY
Document Owner: Operational Risk | Version 2.3 | Last Reviewed: August 2025

1. Purpose
This policy sets out how the firm assesses and manages risk arising from outsourcing and third-party vendor relationships.

2. Vendor Categorisation
2.1 Critical vendors: those supporting a critical business function, e.g. core banking platform, payment processors.
2.2 Important vendors: significant but not critical to continued operations.
2.3 Standard vendors: limited business impact if disrupted.

3. Due Diligence Before Onboarding a Vendor
3.1 Financial stability assessment.
3.2 Information security review, including a completed security questionnaire.
3.3 Data protection assessment where the vendor will process personal data, including a Data Processing Agreement.
3.4 For critical vendors, an on-site or virtual audit is required before contract signature.

4. Ongoing Monitoring
Critical vendors are reviewed annually; important vendors every two years. Reviews assess performance against SLAs, incident history, and continued financial stability.

5. Exit Planning
For all critical vendors, an exit plan must be documented and tested, describing how the firm would transition away from the vendor with minimal disruption.

6. Incident Management
Vendor-related incidents affecting customers or data must be reported to Operational Risk within 24 hours and follow the same escalation path as internal incidents.
