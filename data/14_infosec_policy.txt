Meridian Financial Services Ltd
INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Document Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) | Version 4.2 | Last Reviewed: March 2026

1. Purpose
This policy establishes the framework for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the firm's information assets.

2. Access Control
2.1 Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege, role-based basis.
2.2 Access to customer data requires manager approval and is reviewed quarterly.
2.3 Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems containing customer or financial data.

3. Data Encryption
All customer data must be encrypted at rest (AES-256 or equivalent) and in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher).

4. Incident Response
4.1 Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations team immediately via the internal incident channel.
4.2 Incidents are triaged within 1 hour and classified by severity.
4.3 Severity 1 incidents (data breach or system compromise) trigger the Data Breach Response Procedure.

5. Third-Party Access
External parties requiring system access must sign a confidentiality agreement and are subject to the same access control standards as employees.

6. Security Training
All staff complete annual security awareness training, including phishing simulation exercises.

7. Penetration Testing
Independent penetration testing is conducted at least annually on all customer-facing systems, with findings tracked to remediation.
