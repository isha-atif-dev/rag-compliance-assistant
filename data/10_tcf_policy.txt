Meridian Financial Services Ltd
TREATING CUSTOMERS FAIRLY (TCF) POLICY
Document Owner: Head of Compliance | Version 2.0 | Last Reviewed: December 2025

1. Purpose
This policy embeds fair treatment of customers throughout the product lifecycle, in line with FCA Principle 6 and Consumer Duty requirements.

2. The Six TCF Outcomes
2.1 Customers can be confident they are dealing with a firm where fair treatment is central to culture.
2.2 Products and services are designed to meet the needs of identified customer groups.
2.3 Customers are given clear information before, during, and after point of sale.
2.4 Advice is suitable and takes account of customer circumstances.
2.5 Products perform as customers have been led to expect.
2.6 Customers do not face unreasonable barriers to switch, complain, or claim.

3. Product Governance
New products must go through a Product Approval process assessing target market fit, pricing fairness, and foreseeable harm before launch.

4. Monitoring
TCF outcomes are monitored through customer satisfaction surveys, complaints root cause analysis, and product performance reviews, reported quarterly to the Board.

5. Interaction with Consumer Duty
This policy operates alongside the Consumer Duty Implementation Policy, which sets out enhanced, more prescriptive standards for good customer outcomes.
