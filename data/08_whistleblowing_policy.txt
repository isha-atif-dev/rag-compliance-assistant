Meridian Financial Services Ltd
WHISTLEBLOWING POLICY
Document Owner: Head of Compliance | Version 1.6 | Last Reviewed: September 2025

1. Purpose
This policy encourages staff to raise concerns about suspected wrongdoing, malpractice, or risk without fear of detriment, in line with the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998.

2. What Can Be Reported
Concerns may include: financial malpractice, breaches of law or regulation, danger to health and safety, damage to the environment, or the deliberate concealment of any of the above.

3. How to Raise a Concern
3.1 Concerns can be raised with a line manager, the Head of Compliance, or via the independent, confidential whistleblowing hotline operated by a third-party provider.
3.2 Reports may be made anonymously, though providing contact details assists with investigation.

4. Protection from Detriment
No employee will be penalised, dismissed, or treated unfairly for raising a genuine concern in good faith, even if the concern turns out to be mistaken.

5. Investigation
All reports are reviewed by the Whistleblowing Champion (a Non-Executive Director) within 5 business days. Investigations are conducted confidentially, with findings reported back to the whistleblower where possible.

6. External Reporting
Where internal channels are not appropriate, staff may report concerns directly to the FCA or PRA as prescribed regulators.

7. Governance
The Board receives an annual report summarising whistleblowing cases, themes, and outcomes, without identifying individuals.
