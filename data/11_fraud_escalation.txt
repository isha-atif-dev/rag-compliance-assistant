Meridian Financial Services Ltd
FRAUD DETECTION AND ESCALATION PROCEDURE
Document Owner: Fraud Operations | Version 3.1 | Last Reviewed: June 2026

1. Purpose
This procedure defines how suspected fraudulent activity is identified, escalated, and resolved.

2. Detection
2.1 Automated fraud scoring flags transactions exceeding the firm's risk threshold in real time.
2.2 Staff may also identify suspected fraud through customer reports or manual review.

3. Escalation Tiers
3.1 Tier 1 (low confidence flag): reviewed by a Fraud Analyst within 4 business hours.
3.2 Tier 2 (medium confidence): account is temporarily restricted pending review, customer notified.
3.3 Tier 3 (high confidence / confirmed fraud): account is suspended immediately, funds frozen where possible, and case referred to the Fraud Investigation team.

4. Customer Communication
Customers affected by a fraud hold must be contacted within 24 hours with a clear explanation and expected resolution timeline, unless doing so would compromise an active investigation.

5. Law Enforcement Referral
Confirmed fraud cases above £5,000, or involving organised patterns, are reported to Action Fraud and, where relevant, the National Crime Agency.

6. Interaction with AML
Where fraud is suspected to involve money laundering, the case is jointly handled with the MLRO under the AML Policy, and a SAR is considered.

7. Recovery
The Fraud Operations team pursues recovery of funds where possible, including chargebacks and liaison with receiving banks.
