Meridian Financial Services Ltd
COMPLAINTS HANDLING PROCEDURE
Document Owner: Customer Operations | Version 2.2 | Last Reviewed: May 2026

1. Purpose
This procedure describes the step-by-step handling of a complaint from receipt to closure, supporting the Complaints Handling Policy.

2. Step 1: Logging
Every complaint, however received (phone, email, chat, letter, social media), must be logged in the complaints register within 24 hours, including customer details, complaint category, and channel received.

3. Step 2: Acknowledgement
An acknowledgement is sent to the customer confirming receipt, the assigned case handler, and expected timeline.

4. Step 3: Investigation
The case handler gathers relevant evidence: account records, transaction history, prior correspondence, and relevant policy documents. Where the complaint involves a third party (e.g. a payment scheme), the handler must contact that party for information.

5. Step 4: Decision
The case handler prepares a decision with clear reasoning, referencing the relevant policy or regulation. Decisions on complaints involving redress above £500 require sign-off from a Team Lead.

6. Step 5: Final Response Letter
The final response must clearly state the outcome, the reasoning, any redress offered, and the customer's right to refer to the Financial Ombudsman Service within 6 months if unsatisfied.

7. Step 6: Closure and Logging
The case is closed in the system with full documentation retained per the Records Retention Policy.

8. Vulnerable Customers
Where a complainant is identified as vulnerable, the case is flagged and handled with additional care per the Consumer Duty Implementation Policy, including extended response flexibility where appropriate.
