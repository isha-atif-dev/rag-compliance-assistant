Meridian Financial Services Ltd
CONSUMER DUTY IMPLEMENTATION POLICY
Document Owner: Head of Compliance | Version 1.3 | Last Reviewed: May 2026

1. Purpose
This policy sets out how the firm meets the FCA Consumer Duty, requiring firms to act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers.

2. The Four Outcomes
2.1 Products and Services: designed to meet the needs of identified target markets.
2.2 Price and Value: customers receive fair value relative to the price paid.
2.3 Consumer Understanding: communications equip customers to make informed decisions.
2.4 Consumer Support: customers receive support that meets their needs, including when they want to switch, complain, or cancel.

3. Vulnerable Customers
Staff must identify and appropriately support customers in vulnerable circumstances, including those with health conditions, low financial resilience, or low capability, adapting communication and support accordingly.

4. Fair Value Assessments
Each product undergoes an annual Fair Value Assessment comparing price to the benefits and costs delivered, with results reported to the Product Governance Committee.

5. Monitoring Outcomes
Consumer Duty outcomes are monitored using a dashboard of metrics including complaints trends, cancellation rates, and customer understanding survey results, reported to the Board quarterly.

6. Annual Board Report
The Board reviews and approves an annual Consumer Duty compliance report, confirming the firm is delivering good customer outcomes across all four areas.
