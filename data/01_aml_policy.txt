Meridian Financial Services Ltd
ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) POLICY
Document Owner: Head of Compliance | Version 3.2 | Last Reviewed: March 2026

1. Purpose
This policy sets out Meridian Financial Services Ltd's approach to preventing the firm being used for money laundering or terrorist financing, in accordance with the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, the Terrorism Act 2000, and the Money Laundering Regulations 2017 (as amended).

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties acting on behalf of the firm who are involved in onboarding customers, processing transactions, or handling customer funds.

3. Risk-Based Approach
The firm applies a risk-based approach to AML. Customers are assessed at onboarding and on an ongoing basis using the firm's risk scoring methodology, which considers customer type, geographic risk, product risk, and transaction behaviour.

4. Customer Risk Categories
4.1 Low risk: standard retail customers with straightforward, predictable transaction patterns.
4.2 Medium risk: customers with higher transaction volumes or cross-border activity.
4.3 High risk: politically exposed persons (PEPs), customers from high-risk jurisdictions, or customers exhibiting unusual transaction patterns. High-risk customers require Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) as set out in the Customer Due Diligence Standard.

5. Monitoring and Reporting
5.1 All customer accounts are subject to ongoing transaction monitoring using automated rules and manual review.
5.2 Any employee who suspects money laundering must submit an internal report to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) without delay, using the internal SAR form.
5.3 Employees must not disclose to a customer that a suspicious activity report has been made ("tipping off" is a criminal offence).

6. Record Keeping
All customer due diligence records and transaction records must be retained for a minimum of five years from the end of the business relationship, in line with the Records Retention Policy.

7. Training
All staff must complete AML training within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter. Records of completion are maintained by HR and audited quarterly by Compliance.

8. Governance
The MLRO reports to the Board quarterly on AML matters, including SAR volumes, training completion rates, and any regulatory correspondence.
