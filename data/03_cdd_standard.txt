CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD) STANDARD
Document Owner: Compliance | Version 1.9 | Last Reviewed: February 2026

1. Purpose
This standard defines the three tiers of due diligence applied to customers and when each applies.

2. Simplified Due Diligence (SDD)
Applied where the customer presents a genuinely low risk, e.g. certain regulated public bodies. Requires basic identity confirmation only.

3. Standard Due Diligence (CDD)
The default level applied to all retail and business customers. Includes identity verification, address verification, and sanctions/PEP screening as described in the KYC Procedures.

4. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
Applied to high-risk customers, including PEPs, customers connected to high-risk third countries, and customers where the initial risk assessment flags concerns.
4.1 EDD requires: source of funds verification, source of wealth verification, senior management sign-off before onboarding, and more frequent ongoing monitoring.
4.2 Source of funds documentation may include payslips, bank statements, or business accounts, depending on the customer's declared income source.

5. Escalation
Any Onboarding Analyst uncertain which tier applies must escalate to a Compliance Officer before proceeding. Under no circumstances should an account be activated while due diligence is incomplete.

6. Review Triggers
A customer's due diligence tier may be upgraded at any time following a change in circumstances, a negative media hit, or unusual transaction activity identified by monitoring.
