Meridian Financial Services Ltd
DATA RETENTION AND RECORDS MANAGEMENT POLICY
Document Owner: Compliance | Version 2.1 | Last Reviewed: November 2025

1. Purpose
This policy sets minimum and maximum retention periods for categories of records held by the firm, balancing regulatory requirements with data minimisation obligations under GDPR.

2. Retention Schedule
2.1 Customer due diligence records: 5 years from the end of the business relationship (Money Laundering Regulations 2017).
2.2 Transaction records: 5 years from the date of the transaction.
2.3 Suspicious Activity Reports and related internal records: 5 years, held separately with restricted access.
2.4 Complaints records: 3 years from resolution, per FCA DISP rules.
2.5 Marketing consent records: retained for the duration consent is active, plus 2 years after withdrawal.
2.6 Employee records: 6 years after employment ends, in line with statutory limitation periods.

3. Secure Disposal
Records must be securely destroyed at the end of their retention period using approved secure deletion methods. Disposal must be logged.

4. Legal Holds
Where records are subject to litigation, regulatory investigation, or a legal hold notice, standard retention periods are suspended until the hold is lifted.

5. Responsibilities
Each department is responsible for records within its function. Compliance conducts an annual retention audit across all departments.
