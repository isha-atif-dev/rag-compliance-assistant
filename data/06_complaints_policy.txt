Meridian Financial Services Ltd
COMPLAINTS HANDLING POLICY
Document Owner: Head of Customer Operations | Version 3.0 | Last Reviewed: May 2026

1. Purpose
This policy sets out how the firm handles customer complaints fairly and in accordance with the FCA's Dispute Resolution (DISP) rules.

2. What Constitutes a Complaint
Any expression of dissatisfaction, written or verbal, alleging that the customer has suffered financial loss, material distress, or material inconvenience, is treated as a formal complaint.

3. Acknowledgement and Timelines
3.1 Complaints must be acknowledged within 3 business days.
3.2 A final response must be issued within 8 weeks of receipt.
3.3 If a final response cannot be issued within 8 weeks, the customer must be informed in writing of the reason for the delay and their right to refer the matter to the Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS).

4. Investigation Standards
Complaints must be investigated by someone not directly involved in the original matter where possible. All relevant records, call recordings, and account activity must be reviewed before a decision is issued.

5. Redress
Where a complaint is upheld, appropriate redress may include a financial goodwill payment, fee reversal, or corrective account action, determined case by case in line with FCA guidance on fair redress.

6. Root Cause Analysis
Complaints data is reviewed monthly to identify recurring issues. Where a systemic issue is identified, it is escalated to the relevant product or operations team for remediation.

7. Reporting
Complaints volumes and outcomes are reported to the Board quarterly and to the FCA via the biannual complaints return.
