Meridian Financial Services Ltd
SANCTIONS SCREENING POLICY
Document Owner: MLRO Office | Version 2.0 | Last Reviewed: June 2025

1. Purpose
This policy ensures the firm does not provide services to sanctioned individuals, entities, or jurisdictions, in compliance with UK, EU, and OFAC sanctions regimes as applicable.

2. Screening Requirements
2.1 All customers are screened against relevant sanctions lists (OFSI, UN, EU, OFAC) at onboarding.
2.2 Ongoing screening is performed daily against updated sanctions lists for all active customers.
2.3 Payment transactions are screened in real time before execution.

3. Match Handling
3.1 A potential match ("hit") automatically places the relevant transaction or account action on hold pending review.
3.2 A Sanctions Analyst reviews the hit within 4 business hours to determine if it is a true match or false positive.
3.3 Confirmed true matches are escalated immediately to the MLRO, and funds are frozen where legally required.

4. Reporting
Confirmed sanctions matches must be reported to the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) as required by law.

5. List Updates
The sanctions screening system is updated with new list changes within 24 hours of publication by the relevant authority.
