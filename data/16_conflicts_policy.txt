Meridian Financial Services Ltd
CONFLICTS OF INTEREST POLICY
Document Owner: Compliance | Version 1.5 | Last Reviewed: February 2025

1. Purpose
This policy identifies and manages actual, potential, and perceived conflicts of interest that could damage the interests of customers or the firm.

2. Identifying Conflicts
Conflicts may arise from personal relationships, outside business interests, gifts and hospitality, or situations where staff interests diverge from customer interests.

3. Gifts and Hospitality
3.1 Gifts or hospitality above £50 in value must be declared on the Gifts and Hospitality Register.
3.2 Cash gifts must never be accepted under any circumstances.

4. Outside Business Interests
Staff must declare any outside directorships, consultancy roles, or significant shareholdings that could reasonably give rise to a conflict.

5. Management of Conflicts
Where a conflict cannot be avoided, it must be managed through information barriers, independent oversight, or, where necessary, recusal of the individual from the relevant decision.

6. Register
Compliance maintains a central Conflicts of Interest Register, reviewed quarterly by the Board Risk Committee.
