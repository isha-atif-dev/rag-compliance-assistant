Meridian Financial Services Ltd
KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) PROCEDURES
Document Owner: Onboarding Operations | Version 2.4 | Last Reviewed: January 2026

1. Purpose
These procedures describe the practical steps staff must follow to verify customer identity before an account is opened or activated.

2. Standard Onboarding Steps
2.1 Collect full legal name, date of birth, residential address, and nationality.
2.2 Verify identity using a government-issued photo ID (passport or driving licence) plus one proof of address dated within the last 3 months.
2.3 Run identity verification through the approved third-party eKYC provider.
2.4 Screen the customer against sanctions lists, PEP lists, and adverse media as part of onboarding.

3. Business Customer Onboarding
3.1 Verify the legal entity via Companies House (or equivalent registry for non-UK entities).
3.2 Identify and verify all beneficial owners holding 25% or more of shares or voting rights.
3.3 Obtain a copy of the ownership structure chart for entities with complex or layered ownership.

4. Verification Failures
4.1 If identity cannot be verified through automated checks, the case is escalated to the Manual Review team.
4.2 Manual Review may request additional documentation or, where verification cannot be completed, decline the application.

5. Ongoing Monitoring
5.1 Customer information must be refreshed periodically: every 1 year for high-risk customers, every 3 years for medium-risk, every 5 years for low-risk.
5.2 Significant changes in customer behaviour (e.g. large unexplained deposits) trigger an ad-hoc KYC refresh regardless of the scheduled review date.

6. Roles and Responsibilities
Onboarding Analysts perform initial checks. Manual Review handles escalations. The MLRO has final authority on high-risk or ambiguous cases.
